Admissions

Joining Modern International School

Admissions for the new academic session are open across Nursery to Class 10. Here's how the process works, from first enquiry to your child's first day.

01

Enquiry

Reach out to our admission office with your child's details.

02

Document Verification

Submit birth certificate, previous school records and address proof.

03

Fee Payment

Complete the admission fee to confirm your child's seat.

04

Confirmation

Receive your child's roll number, class and section.
